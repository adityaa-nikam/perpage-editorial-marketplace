# PerPage Visual 8.2 Design Audit & Template Tells

> **Goal**: Replace generic AI template aesthetics with an authentic, human-crafted design language rooted in editorial craftsmanship, proofreading, and publishing standards.

---

## 2. Typography Selection Rationale

- **Primary Display & Headings**: **Source Serif 4** (Variable, optical sizes 8–60pt, weights 400–700). Developed specifically for editorial and long-form reading by Frank Grießhammer at Adobe. It offers real character, open counters, humanist warmth, and deliberate rhythm, perfectly avoiding the cramped look of condensed SaaS fonts.
- **Primary Interface & Body**: **Instrument Sans** (Variable, 400–700). A contemporary humanist grotesk designed for editorial and cultural platforms, offering far more personality and legibility than ubiquitous neutral fonts like Inter, while maintaining high x-height and clear tabular numerals.
- **Data & Micro Details Only**: **JetBrains Mono** / System Mono. Restricted exclusively to prices, page counts, dates, and order IDs. Never used for section eyebrows or decorative labels.
